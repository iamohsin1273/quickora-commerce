# User guide

## Shopping

Browse the category tiles or use the debounced search field. The catalog supports category, brand, maximum price, stock and sort filters. Add items with the card controls; the guest basket is persisted locally. Sign in or create a customer account to check out. Save a manual address, apply one of the demo coupons, select COD or the explicitly non-charging demo payment option and place the order.

Addresses with a Chennai PIN beginning `600` are currently serviceable. An order confirmation is saved to your account; status is persisted and refreshed while viewing the order.

## Operations and delivery

Sign in as the administrator to use `/admin`: view persisted metrics, create/activate products, adjust stock with a reason, transition eligible orders, inspect customers and enable/disable coupons. Use the rider account at `/delivery` for assigned tasks and sequential pickup/delivery status actions.

The included rider account may have no assignment until an administrator assigns an order in the backend. Dispatch availability, live GPS, proof of delivery, earnings and SMS/email notifications are not yet integrated.
